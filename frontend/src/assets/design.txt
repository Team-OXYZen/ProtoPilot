Green (#1D9E75) used for: accent bar, labels, pipeline steps, ProtoPilot table column, stat badges, agent boxes, chat bubbles

No drop shadows except subtle ones on card elements
Illustrations should be flat/line-art style, not 3D or photographic
Maintain clear visual hierarchy: Title > Section Headlines > Body text > Labels
